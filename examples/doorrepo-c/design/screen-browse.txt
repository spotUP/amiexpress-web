Legend
------

  H  Header, 3 rows, painted every keypress.
     Row 1: the door's name and version, and the catalog's total.
     Row 2: the current filter and the count it matches.
     Row 3: separator.
     FREE to restyle; the text is generated, so leave the cells writable.

  B  Panel borders. Drawn today with + - | in cyan. This is the most
     obvious thing to redesign - CP437 line-draw, double lines, shadows.
     The LABEL sits in the top border ("- INSTALLED DOORS -" style).

  L  The catalog list, 16 rows at this size. One archive per row: name,
     size, and markers for installed / has-documentation / has-ads.
     The selected row is drawn in reverse. DOOR-WRITTEN.

  D  The detail pane for the selected archive: its FILE_ID.DIZ, or the
     archive's file listing, or its documentation, depending on the mode
     the user has toggled. DOOR-WRITTEN, and the DIZ inside it is the
     ARTIST'S art from the archive - never re-wrapped, never recoloured.

  F  Footer, 3 rows. The key legend, built to fit the width. DOOR-WRITTEN.
     Restyle the bar; do not lay it out as fixed art.

Notes for the designer
----------------------

- The list is 35% of the width (minimum 18 columns), so at 80 columns it
  is columns 1-28 and the detail pane is 29-80. At 132 columns the list
  grows to 46. Your border art must tile or stretch, not be pixel-fixed.

- Two views hide the list entirely and give the detail pane the whole
  screen: the archive listing and the documentation. See screen-full.txt.

- The one row above the footer is where prompts appear (a filename, a new
  access level, "Delete X? (y/N)"). Keep it plain.
