================================================================
                    BULLETIN SYSTEM HELP
================================================================

The Bulletin System allows you to read important announcements
and information posted by the Sysop and other users.

Available Bulletins:

  1  - Top Uploaders
  2  - Top Downloaders
  6  - Last Callers
  12 - System Information

To read a bulletin, select its number from the menu.

Press Q to quit and return to the main menu.
