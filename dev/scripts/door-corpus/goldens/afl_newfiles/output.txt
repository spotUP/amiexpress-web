Functions help :
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
       [n] ---> shows the filelistings who exist and ask first for the Date
                and than for the List 
       [f] ---> shows the filehelp and ask what list you want
     [f x] ---> shows the filelist direct x = number
     [n ?] ---> for the help
    [n -x] ---> shows  Files x days bevore this Day 
   [n t u] ---> shows the files from this day in the upload dir
   [n t a] ---> shows for files from this day in all dirs
   [n s u] ---> shows new Files in the upload dir
   [n s a] ---> shows new Files from all dirs
[n s u ns] ---> shows new Files in the upload dir Nonstop
[n s a ns] ---> shows new Files from all dirs Nonstop
